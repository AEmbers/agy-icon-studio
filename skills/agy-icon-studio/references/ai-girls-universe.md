# 「AI 娘宇宙」同人画风参考库（AI Girls Universe）

本参考库汇总了社区广泛流传的 **@ZipZipPipe（B 站《大AI和小AI们》）** 大模型拟人化核心视觉锚点，用于保证同一套定制插件图标在画风、线条、色彩和细节位置上高度统一。

> 请仅用于个人桌面美化与非商业交流，并在公开分享时保留对原案创作者 **@ZipZipPipe** 的致谢署名。

---

## 一、通用基础画风指令（所有角色共享）

在向 `image-generator` 派发提示词时，固定携带以下基础约束：

- **Style**: Cute Q-version / chibi 2D anime portrait illustration with crisp, clean dark-purple/indigo anime lineart and bright glossy cel-shading.
- **Framing**: Tightly framed centered head-and-hands bust composition filling ~80–85% of a 1:1 square canvas so every detail remains super clear even when downscaled to 48×48px.
- **Hard Negative Constraints**: Strictly NO text, NO letters, NO Chinese/English characters, NO numbers, NO watermarks, NO UI chrome or square borders.

## 二、核心角色精确视觉锚点

### 1. Gemini 猫娘（适用：Google / Gemini 系、通用、汉化、桌面工具类插件）
- **Hair**: Fluffy periwinkle / lavender-indigo wavy long hair blending into soft pastel pink-purple gradient tips, with a playful curved ahoge sticking up at the crown.
- **Ears**: Matching lavender-indigo cat ears on top of her head with soft pastel pink inner ears and fluffy white inner ear fur tufts.
- **Hair Ornament**: On the **viewer's right side** of her bangs / near her ear, wear the iconic gold-rimmed gradient blue-purple 4-point star (Gemini sparkle) hairclip with a tiny dangling diamond charm.
- **Eyes**: Large watery sparkling **heterochromia** anime eyes — **viewer's left eye pastel pink-purple, viewer's right eye warm amber-gold** — cheerful smile and soft pink blush.
- **Default Outfit**: Dark starry indigo-navy outfit collar with gold trim and a small gold-rimmed 4-point star brooch at the chest.

### 2. GPT 小龙娘（适用：OpenAI / ChatGPT / Codex / Sol 类插件）
- **Hair**: Fluffy flowing silver-white long hair with subtle icy jade-teal shimmer at the tips.
- **Horns**: A pair of cute translucent jade-emerald gradient dragon horns on top of her head (NO cat ears).
- **Eyes & Vibe**: Bright confident emerald-teal eyes, poised cheerful big-sisterly smile, soft blush.
- **Default Outfit**: Dark navy-indigo high-tech collar with emerald-teal and gold trim.

### 3. Claude 大小姐（适用：Anthropic / Claude 评审 / 代码审查类插件；或与 Gemini 猫娘组成双人蜂巢组合）
- **Hair & Ornament**: Fluffy warm apricot-orange long hair with a cute golden sunflower hairclip/accessory on top of her hair.
- **Eyes & Vibe**: Warm amber-brown earnest eyes with a proud, slightly tsundere honor-student expression.
- **Default Outfit**: Cream-white and warm brown academy-style sailor/collar outfit.

### 4. DeepSeek 鲸鱼娘（适用：DeepSeek / 本地推理 / 数据挖掘类插件）
- **Hair & Feature**: Deep sapphire-blue & snow-white gradient hair with cute whale fin hair accessories and subtle blue-white maid ribbon collar, cheerful goofy/laid-back smile.

### 5. Kimi 娘 / Grok 娘（按需拓展）
- **Kimi 娘**：银紫色长发，黑白不对称女仆领结，月亮与星空银饰（避免文字字母）。
- **Grok 娘**：金色长发，暗黑礼服配小巧黑色尖角发饰与星空银芒。
